GPU availability

Vast.ai marketplace (rentable, on-demand offers) and RunPod stock levels, sampled every ~20 minutes. Times shown in Europe/London.

Vast.ai — over time

Select up to 8 GPUs. Colours stay with each GPU while it is selected.

Vast.ai — average by hour of day

Mean of the selected metric for each London hour across the range. Each row is shaded on its own scale (higher = further from the background colour), so read patterns within a GPU, not across.

Vast.ai — available GPUs by region

Average available GPUs per region across the range (hover for the average cheapest $/GPU-hr). Each row is shaded on its own scale.

RunPod — stock level over time

RunPod reports a stock level per GPU type (High / Medium / Low / none), not a count. This is the Pods pool, which serverless draws on, so treat it as a proxy for serverless availability.

RunPod — share of samples with stock, by hour of day

Percentage of samples in each London hour where the GPU showed Low, Medium or High stock.

RunPod — share of samples with stock, by datacenter

Percentage of samples where the GPU showed any stock in that datacenter. A serverless endpoint with a network volume is tied to one datacenter, so this is the view to use when choosing where to put the volume.
